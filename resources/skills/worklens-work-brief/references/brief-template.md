# Default brief

Use this shape only when the user has not supplied a template. Translate headings
to the requested language. The bracketed text describes what to fill in; do not
leave placeholders or example content in the delivered brief.

```markdown
# [Project] · [Start date–end date]

[One or two sentences on the most important supported progress and current state.]

## Main progress

- [Concrete change within the period, with a source link beside the claim.]

## Blockers and decisions needed

- [Explicit unresolved issue, its documented impact, and any recorded decision
  needed. Include an owner or deadline only when established. Link the evidence.]

## Next steps

- [An agreed or proposed next step, retaining that distinction, with its source.
  Include an owner and date only when recorded.]

Coverage: [Exact period and timezone where known; reviewed sources and project
boundaries; retrieval time where known; material limitations.]
```

Keep the brief readable as a short team update. Omit headings that do not help,
and avoid repeating the same fact in the opening and every section. Add a
separately labeled current-status sentence when useful evidence falls outside
the requested period.

A management audience usually needs milestones, material blockers, and decisions.
A delivery team may need issue/PR references and dependencies. In either case,
preserve uncertainties and source links; audience changes do not change facts.

If no in-period progress can be established, say so within the actual coverage.
Summarize any verified current state separately instead of manufacturing progress.
